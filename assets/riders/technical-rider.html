Core requirements

Everything below is essential for the live drum + DJ set to sound the way it's designed to sound. Please confirm all points with your sound engineer ahead of the event.

01 Four microphones (preferably Shure SM57) with stands and cables
02 Several monitor wedges
03 Two keyboard standsThis is essential for our drum setup
04 Sound system appropriate for the venue size
05 Mixer
06 For outdoor events: all microphones must be equipped with proper wind protection (professional-grade foam or furry windshields, e.g. "deadcat")To prevent wind noise and ensure sound quality
Critical point

One sound engineer is required, who will patch and mix everything from the console, be able to mix live drums with DJ music to achieve perfect balance, and must stay in the hall for the entire event, actively managing the sound - adjusting FOH and monitors if anything goes wrong during the performance. The sound engineer must be present at all times.
